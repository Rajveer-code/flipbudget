## Draft AI Use Statement (for the submission; author approves the final wording)

> This work used an AI coding and reasoning assistant (Claude) throughout. The assistant
> derived and wrote up the paper's propositions and their proofs, which the author verified
> against independent brute-force computation and full-scale simulation; proposed parts of the
> method, including the heterogeneity parameter and the falsification comparison; wrote
> essentially all of the analysis code, the labeling tool, the quality gate and the figure and
> macro generators; ran the experiments; investigated the provenance of the benchmark's
> published verdicts, which uncovered both the identity of the published scorer and a
> platform-specific defect in the author's own earlier scoring; and drafted internal documents
> and manuscript prose from the author's outline. It also assisted with literature search and
> with summarising venue requirements.
>
> All human labels in this paper were produced by people. No AI tool generated, pre-filled,
> corrected or edited any label, and the assistant did not see a label before a person typed
> it. Label batches were screened by an automated quality gate, and batches that failed were
> excluded and logged.
>
> The author checked every derivation, re-ran the full pipeline, inspected every figure, and
> traced every number in the paper to a generated macro backed by a committed result file. The
> author is solely responsible for the content of this paper, including any error.
