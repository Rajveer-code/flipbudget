\subsection{Four-layer uncertainty decomposition}
\label{sec:results-decomposition}

Two layers are easy to conflate and are kept structurally separate:
\emph{scorer-identification-only} asks how much $A^\ast$ could vary at a
fixed, exactly-known $(\alpha,\beta)$---it would not shrink with an
infinite audit. \emph{Audit-estimation-only} asks how much not knowing
$(\alpha,\beta)$ precisely widens the set---this shrinks as the audit
grows. Sampling (0.043, $n=136$) $<$ scorer-identification (0.068, $n=66$)
$<$ scorer-choice (0.009, $n=120$) $\ll$ audit-estimation (0.280, $n=120$),
Figure~\ref{fig:decomposition} and Table~\ref{tab:layers}.

\subsection{Flip-budget robustness}
\label{sec:results-flip-budget}

Bootstrapped $d^\ast$ on the current roster's most fragile and most robust
resolved pairs: both require substantial differential error
($d^\ast \geq 0.17$, 95\% intervals excluding zero) to flip---real
comparisons, once identified, are decisively resolved (figure and
per-pair detail: Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-figures}).

\subsection{Scorer-mismatch (established main finding; \exactmatch's own audit pending)}
\label{sec:results-scorer-mismatch}

MATH-Hard's harness computes two live scores per item: \exactmatch\ (the
field's reported metric) and \scoreboxed\ (what every audit round has
measured). \textbf{Full population: 3.63\% disagreement (817/22{,}508, 95\%
CI [3.39\%, 3.88\%]), model range 0.60--9.59\%, bidirectional.}

\textbf{The 0.0091 median ``scorer-choice width'' is an accuracy-substitution
shift---holding \scoreboxed's box fixed, varying only the accuracy---not a
full symmetric two-scorer comparison}, since \exactmatch\ has no audited
box of its own yet (per-pair detail: Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-figures}).

\textbf{Worst-case sensitivity, established without new labels}: bounding
\exactmatch's plausible $\alpha$ at $[2.38\%,26.73\%]$ from the observed
disagreement population sizes and running it through the identical
$\Lambda=2$ methodology gives median width \textbf{0.166}---below the
established audit-estimation median (0.280). \textbf{Verdict B survives
even this bound}, honestly: only 10 of 120 pairs stay valid at all, and all
10 are unresolved (Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-figures} for the figure).
\pending{\exactmatch's actual audited $\alpha,\beta$ and its own
self-consistent result, Analysis C}.

\subsection{Generalization beyond MATH-Hard (limited, not fabricated)}
\label{sec:results-generalization}

IFEval's \code{strict\_acc}/\code{loose\_acc} confirmed as a second, real,
structurally different data point (2.60\% disagreement---two leniency
settings of \emph{one} verifier, not two independent libraries). BBH,
GPQA/MMLU-PRO/MuSR structurally ruled out. \textbf{Confirmed on one
benchmark in the strong sense, a related-but-distinct phenomenon on a
second; not a general property of automated evaluation.}
