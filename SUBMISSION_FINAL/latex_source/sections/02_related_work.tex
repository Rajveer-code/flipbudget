\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\paragraph{Partial identification and misclassification.} The identification
strategy is a direct adaptation of the classical misclassification-bounds
literature \citep{manski1995identification,molinari2008partial}---sensitivity/specificity-style
calibration constants bounding a corrupted binary outcome (class A/B
throughout, Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-novelty}). The contribution is
applying it to benchmark comparison with an audit-design layer classical
misclassification papers do not address.

\paragraph{\citet{chen2026partial}.} Study partial identification of a
\emph{prevalence} from panels of LLM \emph{raters} whose errors are
correlated by shared training lineage---the same toolkit, a structurally
different problem (their LLMs are the noisy raters; ours are the
\emph{rated} models, scored by one fixed, non-LLM verifier). No overlap in
estimand or headline results.

\paragraph{Concurrent work by the same author, sharing empirical
infrastructure.} \citet{pall2026identified} (under review at TMLR, not yet
archival) bounds reported accuracy by a scorer's discarded parse-failure
count $U$ and reports 90.2--94.4\% of 378 MATH-Hard pairwise orderings not
identified. Both papers draw on the \emph{same} 400-item human audit and
28-model roster---disclosed explicitly rather than left for a reader to
find. Distinct estimand (full $(\alpha,\beta)$ box here vs.\ a
parse-failure-conditioned bound there), distinct headline statistic (width
ratio vs.\ \% unidentified), and two contributions absent from that
paper---audit-budget design (Section~\ref{sec:audit-design}) and
scorer-choice dependence (Section~\ref{sec:results-scorer-mismatch}).
Full comparison and policy analysis: \code{VENUE\_AND\_OVERLAP\_AUDIT.md}
in the code release.

\paragraph{Verifier reliability.} VerifyBench \citep{li2025verifybench} and
CompassVerifier \citep{liu2025compassverifier} ask \emph{which verifier is
more accurate}---complementary, not overlapping, with our question (``given
an audited error rate, what can still be concluded''). Hugging Face's own
postmortem \citep{kydlicek2025fixing} independently documents the same
comparator-limitation family this paper's adversarial suite found for
\scoreboxed, and confirms \exactmatch\ is the field's currently-endorsed
replacement metric after the old comparator's failures were severe enough
to force a platform-wide rescoring of 3{,}751 models---external,
production-scale confirmation this is a real problem, not a framing
artifact.

\paragraph{Audit design.} The stratified-allocation solution is
\citet{neyman1934stratified}, unmodified---cited, not claimed novel.
Concurrently, \citet{morikawa2026tsneyman} independently note that
plug-in Neyman degrades under sparse pilots and propose an adaptive
Thompson-sampling fix with formal convergence guarantees on generic
estimation benchmarks---a different problem (a new adaptive algorithm)
from ours (diagnosing the failure, on real audit data, against a discrete
unresolved-pair-count objective specific to benchmark auditing, not
continuous MSE). Our contribution is a documented, tested failure mode of
the plug-in form under sparse real data and a corrected, simple
allocation---not a new algorithm.

\paragraph{Verification bias.} The stratified-on-scorer-outcome design is
shown formally distinct from the classical partial-verification-bias
problem \citep{begg1983assessment}: our sampling probabilities are known by
design, the condition under which Horvitz--Thompson estimation
\citep{horvitz1952generalization} is valid (Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-proofs}).
