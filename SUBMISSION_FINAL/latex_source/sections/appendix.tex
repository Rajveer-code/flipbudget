\subsection{The Horvitz--Thompson / post-stratified estimator, full detail}
\label{sec:appendix-ht}

For a scorer's credited (or wrong) stratum composed of cells $S=\{h\}$ with
population size $N_h$ and sample $(n_h, x_h)$ (scored rows, events):
\begin{align}
  \hat{p}_{\mathrm{HT}} &= \frac{\sum_{h \in S} N_h \cdot (x_h/n_h)}{\sum_{h \in S} N_h} \\
  \mathrm{Var}(\hat{p}_{\mathrm{HT}}) &= \frac{1}{\left(\sum_h N_h\right)^2}
    \sum_h N_h^2 \left(1 - \frac{n_h}{N_h}\right) \frac{\hat{p}_h(1-\hat{p}_h)}{\max(n_h-1,\,1)}
\end{align}
$(1-n_h/N_h)$ is the finite-population correction. This estimator was
adopted after a naive pooled-proportion estimator was found, by simulation,
to be biased by $+444\%$ relative to the design-weighted truth in a
realistic scenario where a small, densely-oversampled cell's true rate
differs from the majority cell's---exactly the scenario stratifying on
disagreement was designed to detect. Seven regression tests, including a
parametrized synthetic-recovery check at four different
majority/minority-rate pairs, cover this estimator directly.

\subsection{Exact metric definitions}
\label{sec:appendix-metrics}

\begin{itemize}
  \item $\alpha$ (false-credit rate): $P(\text{scorer says correct} \mid \text{true label incorrect})$.
  \item $\beta$ (false-miss rate): $P(\text{scorer says incorrect} \mid \text{true label correct})$.
  \item Identified-set width: $\mathrm{hi}-\mathrm{lo}$ of the sharp interval for $A^\ast$ or $\Delta^\ast$.
  \item Dominant: $W_{\text{identification}} / W_{\text{sampling}} > 1$ for a given pair.
  \item Unresolved: the identified interval for $\Delta^\ast$ contains 0.
  \item Flip budget $d^\ast$: the minimum differential-error perturbation
  (beyond the audited margins) required to make an otherwise-resolved
  comparison's sign ambiguous.
  \item Scorer-disagreement rate: fraction of (model, item) rows where
  \scoreboxed\ and \exactmatch\ give different binary verdicts on the
  identical raw response.
\end{itemize}
