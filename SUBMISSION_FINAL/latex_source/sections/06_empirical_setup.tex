\section{Empirical Setup}
\label{sec:empirical-setup}

\paragraph{Data.} Open LLM Leaderboard v2's MATH-Hard subtask, 7 subjects, 27
models with public per-item generations (17 pass the audit-qualification
gate: nonzero audit $n$ on both the credited and wrong strata), drawn from a
base candidate roster of 28 models (Table~\ref{tab:dataset}). Standard
comparator: the leaderboard harness's own math-scoring utilities, verified
byte-identical to a companion project's copy.

\paragraph{Original human audit (Analyses A/B's basis---the source of every
headline number in this paper).} 400 items submitted against the
\scoreboxed\ (last-\code{\textbackslash boxed\{\}}-found) extraction path,
350 usable across 27 models (162 in the credited stratum, 188 in the wrong
stratum; the remainder indeterminate/unparseable, held out).
Empirical-Bayes-pooled per-model $\alpha$: 0.0314; $\beta \approx 0$,
verified against the already-published pooled figure before any per-model
breakdown was trusted. This is the data every result in
Sections~\ref{sec:results-dominance}--\ref{sec:results-flip-budget} and
Figures~\ref{fig:decomposition}--\ref{fig:identified-set},
\ref{fig:worst-case}--\ref{fig:flip-budget} is built on.

\paragraph{T-C round (a separate, later, additional audit---not yet merged
into the pipeline above).} 69 usable, blinded, transcription-protocol-labeled
human judgments, targeted at the highest-priority (model, stratum) cells
identified by the audit-design analysis (Section~\ref{sec:audit-design}),
also against \scoreboxed. 0/69 scorer-wrong events; 100\% three-way
inter-rater agreement (14-item overlap). Folding these 69 items into the
pooling analytically shifts the global anchor from 0.0314 to 0.0216 and
expands qualification from 17 to 25 models---computed as a standalone
comparison, but this updated pooling has \textbf{not} been propagated back
into the canonical headline pipeline; every number in
Section~\ref{sec:results} still reflects the pre-T-C-round pooling. Flagged
as a limitation (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}), not silently left
inconsistent.

\paragraph{Second, larger human audit (Analysis C's basis, in the field).}
458 rows, jointly stratified on both \scoreboxed\ and \exactmatch,
preregistered before generation, power-justified ($n=300$ for a first
\exactmatch-$\alpha$ estimate; $n=60$, 65--89\% power, for a
scorer-disagreement-resolution comparison). \pending{the full analysis}.

\paragraph{Excluded, not used.} Two prior labeling-quality failures (a
corrupted resubmission; two failed IFEval rounds) and \textbf{two
AI-generated substitutions for the second T-C round}, all detected via a
statistical-signature check (near-total mechanical agreement with automated
scorers, zero use of the ``genuinely ambiguous'' marking, implausible
turnaround time, and---for the second attempt---a forensic diff showing 318
of 458 rows changed by one uniform mechanical bulk edit while introducing
new corruption rather than fixing anything) and preserved as provenance
only, never entering any result.
