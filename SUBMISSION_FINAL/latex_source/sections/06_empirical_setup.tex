\section{Empirical Setup}
\label{sec:empirical-setup}

\paragraph{Data.} Open LLM Leaderboard v2's MATH-Hard subtask, 7 subjects,
27 models with public per-item generations (17 pass the audit-qualification
gate: nonzero audit $n$ on both strata), from a base candidate roster of 28
models, 378 candidate pairs (Table~\ref{tab:dataset}).

\paragraph{Original human audit (Analyses A/B's basis).} 350 usable of 400
items submitted against \scoreboxed\ (162 credited, 188 wrong).
Empirical-Bayes-pooled $\alpha=0.0314$, $\beta\approx0$. This is the data
every result in Section~\ref{sec:results} is built on, and the audit shared
with \citet{pall2026identified} (Section~\ref{sec:related-work}).

\paragraph{T-C round (separate, later, not yet merged).} 69 usable,
blinded judgments targeted at the highest-priority cells identified by
audit design, also against \scoreboxed. 0/69 scorer-wrong; folding these in
analytically shifts the anchor to 0.0216 and qualification to 25
models---computed as a standalone comparison, \textbf{not} propagated into
the pipeline above (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).

\paragraph{Second, larger human audit (Analysis C's basis).} 458 rows,
jointly stratified on \scoreboxed\ and \exactmatch, preregistered,
power-justified. \pending{the full analysis}.

\paragraph{Excluded, not used.} Prior labeling-quality failures and
\textbf{three AI-generated substitutions} for this round, all detected via
a statistical-signature check (near-total mechanical agreement with the
automated scorers, near-zero use of the ambiguity marker, and---the third
attempt---filesystem timestamps showing the entire 458-row file existed for
under two minutes before submission) and preserved as provenance only,
never entering any result.
